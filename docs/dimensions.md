# Dimensions research-system signals

Dimensions includes publications, datasets, grants, patents, clinical trials, policy documents and technical reports, with cross-domain links that complement conventional article-level metrics. See https://help.dimensions.ai/en/articles/9780225 and https://help.dimensions.ai/en/articles/9780545 .

Additional candidate outcomes: funding-to-publication/trial/patent lag; award/organisation/funder portfolios; patent-family science links; clinical trial-linked publication trajectories; policy document citation paths; translational pathway attrition; data/open science linkage; classification/taxonomy dependence; geographical and institutional diversity.

Each relationship has provenance, provider source coverage, observation date, confidence when justified, and legal/redistribution rights. **Associations are not causal outcomes.** Dates must be first-observed vs effective where possible, supporting historical forecasting.

FCR/RCR are provider-defined metric observations and shall not be reimplemented under those names without exact methodology/denominator. Metadata and calculated metrics may refresh at different cadences. Source entitlement must be explicit; local-only and public-data paths must remain usable without a Dimensions subscription.

Sources: https://help.dimensions.ai/en/articles/9783745 ; https://help.dimensions.ai/en/articles/9780865 ; https://help.dimensions.ai/en/articles/9780801 .
