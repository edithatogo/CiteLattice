# Product guidelines

- Scientific accuracy, explicit uncertainty and source coverage take precedence
  over cosmetic completeness. Never invent absent citations or impute missing
  values as zeros. Distinguish source, method, result, scene and render identities.
- Keep canonical CSL-JSON independent of projections. Deny network, remote
  inference, model downloads and restricted disclosures by default.
- Prefer accessible exact-value tables alongside figures; no hidden disclosure,
  invented quantitative geometry or unsupported semantic downgrade.
- Use precise, restrained documentation. Label a proposal, scaffold, synthetic
  test, hosted CI result and independently qualified scientific method distinctly.
- Prefer small composable Rust APIs, stable schemas, evidence manifests and
  established optional dependencies. Do not duplicate ecosystem ownership.
