# CiteLattice product definition

CiteLattice is the Rust-native, contract-first successor to the bibx design.2
prototype for bibliometrics, scientometrics and research-system mapping. It
serves researchers and analysts who need auditable measures, versioned inputs,
evidence-linked figures and reproducible results packages.

Searchright owns acquisition and review; Sourceright owns citation integrity;
CiteWeft owns extraction; Innovate and Voiage own forecasting and decision
analysis. CiteLattice owns scientific analytical methods, entity/graph analytics
and table/scene/report contracts. Use optional versioned interfaces, not coupled
deployments. See [requirements](requirements.md), [architecture](design.md) and
[capability matrix](../docs/capabilities.md).

Success means exact provenance and rights, scoped known-answer numerical tests,
independent comparisons, accessible evidence-linked exports and transparent
limitations. Method parity, scientific superiority, live producer compatibility
and publication readiness are separate qualification gates, never inferred from
schemas or a passing general CI workflow.
